# Requested songs awaiting a distribution basis

Updated 2026-10-03. The implemented additions use older underlying melodies
and independently encoded chart exercises. None relies on a fair-use claim.
The traditional selections associated with Pete Seeger, Joan Baez and Woody
Guthrie are listed in [FOLK_ARTISTS_PLAN.md](FOLK_ARTISTS_PLAN.md).

A chart still reproduces musical composition even without lyrics or audio.
Noncommercial, open-source teaching is relevant context, but does not itself
establish permission to distribute the chart. There is no fixed number of
notes, bars, seconds or percentage that automatically makes an excerpt fair
use. See the [US Copyright Office's four-factor explanation](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/).

Luxembourg has specifically enumerated exceptions with individual conditions;
US fair use does not automatically supply a Luxembourg distribution basis.
The [Luxembourg Ministry's guidance](https://meco.gouvernement.lu/en/domaines-activites/propriete-intellectuelle/droits-auteur-voisin.html)
explains this distinction and the general life-plus-70 term. No applicable
exception has been established for unrestricted public distribution of these
requested copyrighted charts. Their playable versions are therefore pending,
including short excerpts. This is not a determination that no exception could
ever apply to a particular teaching use.

For a permission-based release, document permission covering the selected
melody excerpt, harmonica adaptation/transposition, game and repository
distribution, territories, and the permitted downstream redistribution terms.
Keep the composition's license separate from the MIT license on our code.
Any later use of a commercial recording needs its own rights assessment.
