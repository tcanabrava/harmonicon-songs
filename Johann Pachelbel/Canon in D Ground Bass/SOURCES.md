# Canon in D Ground Bass

Plan ID: CL08. Research date: 2026-10-03.

Underlying melody: Pachelbel (1653–1706), Canon ground bass.

Melody reference: [Original Canon and Gigue score catalog](https://imslp.org/wiki/Canon_and_Gigue_in_D_major,_P.37_(Pachelbel,_Johann)). Original bass sequence D–A–B–F-sharp–G–D–G–A. Uniform octave transposition preserves its original intervals; no variation or modern arrangement is claimed.

Adaptation: The complete eight-note ground-bass pattern, transposed from D to C and raised to holes 4–7. Three practice passes; this is the bass, not the violin melody or complete Canon.

The chart uses a standard C Richter harmonica, 64 BPM, 4/4, C, single notes only, and no bends. A four-beat count-in precedes the first note; repeated passes have two beats of breathing space. Upper-register exercises need musical and physical play-through before release.

Rights scope: the older underlying melody is treated as public domain; this independently encoded beginner exercise is MIT licensed. Reference editions, source MIDI performances, modern harmony, lyrics, recordings, and artwork are not included or licensed by this chart. No backing audio is supplied.
