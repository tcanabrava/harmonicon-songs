# Oh Susanna

Plan ID: RK03. Research date: 2026-10-02.

Underlying melody: Stephen Foster, 1848; composer died 1864.

Melody reference: [University of Pittsburgh: historical Oh! Susanna score](https://americanmusic.library.pitt.edu/content/oh-susanna). The University of Pittsburgh’s American Music Collection provides the historical published score. The 1848 composition is beyond life-plus-70 terms in Luxembourg/EU and Brazil and beyond the US publication term. This is a melody exercise, not a modern folk or rock recording.

Adaptation: Opening verse melody, transposed into C5–A5 with straight slow eighth notes and one-beat phrase endings. Two practice passes; all lyrics and accompaniment omitted.

The chart uses a standard C Richter harmonica, 76 BPM, 2/4, C, single notes only, and no bends. A four-beat count-in precedes the first note; repeated passes have two beats of breathing space. Upper-register exercises need musical and physical play-through before release.

Rights scope: the older underlying melody is treated as public domain; this independently encoded beginner exercise is MIT licensed. Reference editions, source MIDI performances, modern harmony, lyrics, recordings, and artwork are not included or licensed by this chart. No backing audio is supplied.
