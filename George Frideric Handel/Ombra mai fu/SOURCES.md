# Ombra mai fu

Plan ID: CL07. Research date: 2026-10-03.

Underlying melody: Handel (1685–1759), Serse (1738).

Melody reference: [Sibley Library original 1905 score](https://hdl.handle.net/1802/16201). Songs and Airs, Oliver Ditson 1905, edited Ebenezer Prout (1835–1909), printed pages 72–73 (scan pages 96–97). The library explicitly identifies this edition as public domain. Only the original vocal line is encoded, not the translated lyrics or piano reduction.

Adaptation: Opening vocal phrase in F transposed to C. Initial six-beat tied hold shortened to three beats; dotted-eighth/sixteenth pair changed to two quarters. One beat of breath added after the phrase; three passes.

The chart uses a standard C Richter harmonica, 60 BPM, 3/4, C, single notes only, and no bends. A four-beat count-in precedes the first note; repeated passes have two beats of breathing space. Upper-register exercises need musical and physical play-through before release.

Reference editions, source MIDI performances, modern harmony, lyrics, recordings, and artwork are not included or licensed by this chart. No backing audio is supplied.
