# Terezinha de Jesus

Plan ID: BR08. Research date: 2026-10-02.

Underlying melody: Anonymous traditional melody.

Melody reference: [Traditional melody reference](https://www.mamalisa.com/?t=es&p=212). The Terezinha tune family appears in [Cantigas das Creanças e do Povo (1911)](https://digital.bbm.usp.br/handle/bbm/7999), printed pages 64–65; collector Alexina Magalhães Pinto died 1921. The chart selects the traditional E-minor variant in the linked monophonic reference.

Adaptation: Opening two phrases only, E minor to A minor in the upper octave (A5–F6); durations doubled. Two practice passes; remaining verse omitted to keep the range smaller.

The chart uses a standard C Richter harmonica, 64 BPM, 2/4, Am, single notes only, and no bends. A four-beat count-in precedes the first note; repeated passes have two beats of breathing space. Upper-register exercises need musical and physical play-through before release.

Rights scope: the older underlying melody is treated as public domain; this independently encoded beginner exercise is MIT licensed. Reference editions, source MIDI performances, modern harmony, lyrics, recordings, and artwork are not included or licensed by this chart. No backing audio is supplied. Genre labels identify planned pack styles; bossa nova, jazz, blues, and rock accompaniment remains future work.
