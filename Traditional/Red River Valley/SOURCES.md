# Red River Valley

Plan ID: CY02. Research date: 2026-10-02.

Underlying melody: Anonymous traditional melody.

Melody reference: [Musica Viva / John Chambers traditional ABC](https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/abc/mirror/musicaviva.com/usa/red-river-valley/0000). Anonymous nineteenth-century North American tune, listed in the American Song Treasury public-domain repertoire. The chart uses the linked traditional melody reference rather than a named modern performer’s arrangement. Associated performances: Arlo Guthrie, Son of the Wind (1992), and Woody Guthrie’s folk repertoire. Their masters and interpretations are not included. Performance evidence: [Arlo album catalog](https://music.amazon.ca/albums/B010DD4ITW), [Woody track catalog](https://folkways.si.edu/woody-guthrie/red-river-valley/american-folk-childrens-struggle-protest/music/track/smithsonian). [Smithsonian historical liner notes](https://folkways-media.si.edu/docs/folkways/artwork/SFW40103.pdf) discuss the older song and early recordings.

Adaptation: Complete traditional verse in G, raised an octave (D5–D6). One chromatic passing E-flat in the reference is omitted by holding its preceding E for one beat. Tied notes combined; no modern harmony.

The chart uses a standard C Richter harmonica, 68 BPM, 4/4, G, single notes only, and no bends. A four-beat count-in precedes the first note; repeated passes have two beats of breathing space. Upper-register exercises need musical and physical play-through before release.

Rights scope: the older underlying melody is treated as public domain; this independently encoded beginner exercise is MIT licensed. Reference editions, source MIDI performances, modern harmony, lyrics, recordings, and artwork are not included or licensed by this chart. No backing audio is supplied. Genre labels identify planned pack styles; bossa nova, jazz, blues, and rock accompaniment remains future work.
