# Oh Freedom

Plan ID: FA07. Research date: 2026-10-03.

Underlying melody: Anonymous American spiritual, nineteenth century.

Melody reference: [Anonymous spiritual ABC reference](https://trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/abc/mirror/musicaviva.com/tunes/usa/oh-freedom.abc). Reference ABC X:553 identifies anonymous USA spiritual. Opening melody only, excluding later civil-rights verses and performance-specific arrangements. Joan Baez’s official Oh, Freedom release credits the composition Traditional: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hWgdx3qpdIo . Pete’s repertoire connection is documented in Smithsonian SFW40225 notes: https://folkways-media.si.edu/docs/folkways/artwork/SFW40225.pdf .

Adaptation: Opening four bars, D major transposed to C two octaves above the source’s low register; holes 6–8. Two passes. A traditional-repertoire addition associated with Joan Baez and Pete Seeger; no artist arrangement copied.

The chart uses a standard C Richter harmonica, 60 BPM, 4/4, C, single notes only, and no bends. A four-beat count-in precedes the first note; repeated passes have two beats of breathing space. Upper-register exercises need musical and physical play-through before release.

Rights scope: the older underlying melody is treated as public domain; this independently encoded beginner exercise is MIT licensed. Reference editions, source MIDI performances, modern harmony, lyrics, recordings, and artwork are not included or licensed by this chart. No backing audio is supplied.
