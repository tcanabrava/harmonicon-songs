# Oh My Darling Clementine

Plan ID: CY03. Research date: 2026-10-02.

Underlying melody: Traditional nineteenth-century tune; commonly associated with Percy Montrose’s 1884 song.

Melody reference: [Digital Tradition / John Chambers monophonic ABC](https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/abc/mirror/back.numachi.com/Clementine/0000). The melody is older traditional American repertoire, rather than a transcription of Pete Seeger’s recording. The historical 1884 song is beyond US publication terms. Associated performance: Pete Seeger’s American folk repertoire. No performer’s recording, new harmonization, or lyrics is included. [Smithsonian’s historical liner notes](https://folkways-media.si.edu/docs/folkways/artwork/SFW40150.pdf) identify the 1863 Barker and 1884 Montrose publications; [Pete’s track catalog](https://folkways.si.edu/pete-seeger/clementine-2/american-folk/music/track/smithsonian) verifies the performance connection. Historical anonymous-tune attribution is distinguished from the published song and later arrangements.

Adaptation: Complete traditional melody, D to C (G4–G5). Dotted-eighth/sixteenth pairs equalized to eighths. Two practice passes.

The chart uses a standard C Richter harmonica, 68 BPM, 3/4, C, single notes only, and no bends. A four-beat count-in precedes the first note; repeated passes have two beats of breathing space. Upper-register exercises need musical and physical play-through before release.
