# Marcha Soldado

Plan ID: BR04. Research date: 2026-10-02.

Underlying melody: Anonymous traditional melody.

Melody reference: [UFG: Brincando e Aprendendo, printed page 33](https://files.cercomp.ufg.br/weby/up/269/o/Brincando_e_Aprendendo.compressed.pdf). The university educational collection labels this a popular song and prints the familiar monophonic C-major melody. [Discos do Brasil](https://discografia.discosdobrasil.com.br/compositor/dominio-publico) attributes Marcha Soldado to Domínio Público. No lyrics, teaching text, engraving, or accompaniment are copied.

Adaptation: Opening eight-bar phrase, raised an octave to C5–A5; the final tonic is held for a half note and the next-verse pickup omitted. Two practice passes.

The chart uses a standard C Richter harmonica, 76 BPM, 2/4, C, single notes only, and no bends. A four-beat count-in precedes the first note; repeated passes have two beats of breathing space. Upper-register exercises need musical and physical play-through before release.

Reference editions, source MIDI performances, modern harmony, lyrics, recordings, and artwork are not included or licensed by this chart. No backing audio is supplied.
