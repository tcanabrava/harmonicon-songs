# Amazing Grace

Checked: 2026-10-02. Existing chart retained.

The tune is New Britain, paired with Amazing Grace in William Walker’s Southern Harmony (1835), as documented by the [Library of Congress timeline](https://www.loc.gov/collections/amazing-grace/articles-and-essays/timeline). This historical tune is public domain; the modern Joan Baez recording and arrangement are separate works.

[A&M’s Joan Baez press release](https://www.onamrecords.com/sites/default/files/2020-08/602498_604793_PR.pdf) identifies Amazing Grace on From Every Stage. This is a repertoire association, not a transcription claim. No modern singer lyrics or recording are included.

The existing C-harmonica chart uses holes 6–9 at 80 BPM. It passes pack validation; a physical beginner play-through remains pending.
