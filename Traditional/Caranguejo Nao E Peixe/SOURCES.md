# Caranguejo Nao E Peixe

Plan ID: FR02. Research date: 2026-10-03.

Underlying melody: Anonymous Brazilian traditional cantiga.

Melody reference: [UFSCar folklore vocal-melody reference](https://www.servidores.ufscar.br/glauber/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/Caranguelo-CD-Musicaliza-Grade.pdf). The score explicitly credits Folclore brasileiro. Only its traditional vocal line is used, not Glauber Santiago’s 2002 ensemble arrangement.

Adaptation: Opening vocal excerpt, pickup before bar 9 through bar 15; raised one octave and durations doubled. Modern ensemble introduction and accompaniment omitted.

The chart uses a standard C Richter harmonica, 72 BPM, 2/4, C, single notes only, and no bends. A four-beat count-in precedes the first note; repeated passes have two beats of breathing space. Upper-register exercises need musical and physical play-through before release.
