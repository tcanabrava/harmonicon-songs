# Sapo Cururu

Plan ID: BR03. Research date: 2026-10-02.

Underlying melody: Anonymous traditional melody.

Melody reference: [Traditional melody reference](https://www.mamalisa.com/?t=es&p=4826). The older Sapo Jururu tune family is printed in Alexina Magalhães Pinto’s [Cantigas das Creanças e do Povo (1911)](https://digital.bbm.usp.br/handle/bbm/7999), printed page 41; collector died 1921. The selected modern reference is the traditional variant, not Gentil Puget’s distinct composition. Traditional attribution also appears in the Brazilian public-domain catalog.

Adaptation: Opening two phrases only; G to C transposition in the upper register (E5–C6), with all durations doubled. Two practice passes.

The chart uses a standard C Richter harmonica, 64 BPM, 2/4, C, single notes only, and no bends. A four-beat count-in precedes the first note; repeated passes have two beats of breathing space. Upper-register exercises need musical and physical play-through before release.

Rights scope: the older underlying melody is treated as public domain; this independently encoded beginner exercise is MIT licensed. Reference editions, source MIDI performances, modern harmony, lyrics, recordings, and artwork are not included or licensed by this chart. No backing audio is supplied. Genre labels identify planned pack styles; bossa nova, jazz, blues, and rock accompaniment remains future work.
