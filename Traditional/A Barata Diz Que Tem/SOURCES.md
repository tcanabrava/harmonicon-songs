# A Barata Diz Que Tem

Plan ID: FR05. Research date: 2026-10-03.

Underlying melody: Anonymous Brazilian traditional cantiga.

Melody reference: [Mama Lisa traditional melody reference](https://www.mamalisa.com/?c=35&p=4412&t=es). Pitches follow barata.mid melody track. The new chart uses no source recording, MIDI bytes, lyrics or accompaniment.

Adaptation: One verse and refrain, raised one octave; MIDI articulation gaps replaced with the notated half-beat/beat slots. No backing supplied.

The chart uses a standard C Richter harmonica, 68 BPM, 2/4, C, single notes only, and no bends. A four-beat count-in precedes the first note; repeated passes have two beats of breathing space. Upper-register exercises need musical and physical play-through before release.
