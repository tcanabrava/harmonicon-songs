# Shenandoah

Plan ID: FA08. Research date: 2026-10-03.

Underlying melody: Anonymous nineteenth-century American sea shanty.

Melody reference: [John Chambers anonymous sea-shanty ABC variant](https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/abc/song/Shenandoah_G/0000). Reference Shenandoah [G], Sea shanty, composer anon.; independently encoded melody. This is the sea shanty, not the unrelated York Fusiliers march also titled Shenandoah. Pete Seeger’s repertoire connection: https://folkways.si.edu/pete-seeger-collection/shenandoah .

Adaptation: Opening four phrases with pickup, G transposed to C. The reference has flexible bar lengths; 4/4 is the practice grid, not a claim that the source is strictly metered. Two passes; no artist recording or accompaniment.

The chart uses a standard C Richter harmonica, 60 BPM, 4/4, C, single notes only, and no bends. A four-beat count-in precedes the first note; repeated passes have two beats of breathing space. Upper-register exercises need musical and physical play-through before release.

Rights scope: the older underlying melody is treated as public domain; this independently encoded beginner exercise is MIT licensed. Reference editions, source MIDI performances, modern harmony, lyrics, recordings, and artwork are not included or licensed by this chart. No backing audio is supplied.
