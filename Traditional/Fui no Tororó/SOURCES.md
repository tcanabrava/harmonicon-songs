# Fui no Tororó

Plan ID: BR12. Research date: 2026-10-02.

Underlying melody: Anonymous traditional melody.

Melody reference: [Anonymous folk melody, preview upper staff](https://www.superpartituras.com.br/-desconhecido-/fui-no-tororo). The source attributes the melody to an unknown author. [Discos do Brasil](https://discografia.discosdobrasil.com.br/compositor/dominio-publico) lists the traditional title as public domain. The folk melody predates Villa-Lobos’s 1926 Cirandas treatment; none of his piano arrangement is used.

Adaptation: Opening traditional verse through its final held note, raised an octave to C5–C6. Following-verse pickup omitted; no lower-staff harmony copied. Straight melody exercise; bossa backing is not supplied.

The chart uses a standard C Richter harmonica, 68 BPM, 2/4, C, single notes only, and no bends. A four-beat count-in precedes the first note; repeated passes have two beats of breathing space. Upper-register exercises need musical and physical play-through before release.

Rights scope: the older underlying melody is treated as public domain; this independently encoded beginner exercise is MIT licensed. Reference editions, source MIDI performances, modern harmony, lyrics, recordings, and artwork are not included or licensed by this chart. No backing audio is supplied.
