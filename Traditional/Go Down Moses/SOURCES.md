# Go Down Moses

Plan ID: BL03. Research date: 2026-10-02.

Underlying melody: Anonymous traditional melody.

Melody reference: [Jubilee Songs (1872), printed page 22](https://archive.org/details/jubileesongscomp00sewa). Jubilee Songs, as Sung by the Jubilee Singers, 1872, printed page 22 (PDF page 24), is the exact historical melody basis. The old anonymous spiritual predates modern blues arrangements. Only the melody of the opening refrain is encoded; historical lower voices are omitted; lyric text is documented below.

Adaptation: First four bars of the historical refrain through “Egypt land”, transposed F minor to A minor in the upper register (A5–E6). Two practice passes. The remaining refrain, including its chromatic final response, is omitted.

The chart uses a standard C Richter harmonica, 60 BPM, 4/4, Am, single notes only, and no bends. A four-beat count-in precedes the first note; repeated passes have two beats of breathing space. Upper-register exercises need musical and physical play-through before release.

Reference editions, source MIDI performances, modern harmony, modern lyrics, recordings, and artwork are not included or licensed by this chart. No backing audio is supplied.
