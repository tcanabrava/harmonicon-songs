# House of the Rising Sun

Plan ID: RK01. Research date: 2026-10-02.

Underlying melody: Anonymous traditional melody.

Melody reference: [Traditional melody illustrated in ABC notation](https://www.phasenumkehr.de/musik/musiknotation). The linked notation identifies the tune as traditional. [Association for Cultural Equity’s historical songbook](https://culturalequity.org/sites/default/files/2022-02/POPULAR%20SONGBOOK%20booklet.pdf) documents the older collected folk song. This is a short independent exercise from the traditional opening, not the Animals’ arrangement or any recording.

Adaptation: Opening verse phrase only (“There is a house in New Orleans”), raised an octave in A minor (A5–E6), repeated three times. The next-phrase pickup is omitted. Uses the traditional triple-meter melody; later chromatic response and modern instrumental arrangement are omitted.

The chart uses a standard C Richter harmonica, 60 BPM, 3/4, Am, single notes only, and no bends. A four-beat count-in precedes the first note; repeated passes have two beats of breathing space. Upper-register exercises need musical and physical play-through before release.

Rights scope: the older underlying melody is treated as public domain; this independently encoded beginner exercise is MIT licensed. Reference editions, source MIDI performances, modern harmony, lyrics, recordings, and artwork are not included or licensed by this chart. No backing audio is supplied. Genre labels identify planned pack styles; bossa nova, jazz, blues, and rock accompaniment remains future work.
