# Home on the Range

Plan ID: CY01. Research date: 2026-10-02.

Underlying melody: Daniel E. Kelley (1843–1905); nineteenth-century melody.

Melody reference: [Musica Viva / John Chambers monophonic ABC](https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/abc/mirror/musicaviva.com/tunes/usa/home-on-the-range/0000). The underlying Kelley melody is outside life-plus-70 terms; no modern harmonization or lyrics are included. [Song of America composer biography](https://www.songofamerica.net/composer/kelley-daniel) supplies Kelley’s dates.

Adaptation: Opening eight bars of the chorus only, G to C (G4–G5). Dotted quarter/eighth pairs made equal quarters. The lower-register part of the remaining chorus is omitted. Two practice passes.

The chart uses a standard C Richter harmonica, 66 BPM, 3/4, C, single notes only, and no bends. A four-beat count-in precedes the first note; repeated passes have two beats of breathing space. Upper-register exercises need musical and physical play-through before release.
