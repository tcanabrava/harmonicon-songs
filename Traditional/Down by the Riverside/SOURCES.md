# Down by the Riverside

Plan ID: JZ02. Research date: 2026-10-02.

Underlying melody: Anonymous traditional melody.

Melody reference: [Musica Viva / John Chambers monophonic ABC](https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/abc/mirror/musicaviva.com/tunes/usa/down-by-the-riverside/0000). Traditional African-American spiritual, printed in Plantation Melodies (1918). [Library of Congress historical notes](https://lcweb2.loc.gov/static/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/documents/DownByTheRiverside.pdf) describe its older roots; no Sister Rosetta Tharpe recording or arrangement is used.

Adaptation: Chorus only, using the final tonic ending, transposed D to C. All rhythmic values doubled; tied notes combined. Repeated verses and chromatic pickups are omitted. Jazz accompaniment is not supplied.

The chart uses a standard C Richter harmonica, 72 BPM, 2/4, C, single notes only, and no bends. A four-beat count-in precedes the first note; repeated passes have two beats of breathing space. Upper-register exercises need musical and physical play-through before release.
