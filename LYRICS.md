# Lyrics coverage

Checked: 2026-10-03. 47 vocal charts include public-domain original-language text excerpts.

Lyrics attach to note onsets with `track[].lyric`. A trailing `-` joins syllables; a leading `/` starts a line; an unannotated note sustains the preceding lyric. These charts declare format **1.6.0**. Count-ins and rests carry no words. Practice passes repeat the same excerpt.

Some beginner arrangements reduce the original vocal notes. Their annotations group a word or phrase on an onset, and the game highlights that group together. The table distinguishes phrase display from syllable display; each source note records any grouped syllables. These are teaching excerpts, rather than complete-song lyric sheets or transcriptions of the named modern performers.

Text rights are considered separately from melody rights: named historical authors and anonymous traditional texts are used in their original languages. No modern translation or newly written performer verse is included. Anonymous folk texts have variants; the linked reference identifies the selected words, and spelling normalization or variant choices are recorded in `SOURCES.md`. The source website itself, its commentary, translations, recordings and arrangements are not licensed by this repository.

## Passages without lyrics

- The instrumental Bach *Jesu, Joy of Man’s Desiring* passage, the symphony themes, Pachelbel’s bass pattern, *Für Elise*, *Toccata*, and *O Pulo da Gaita* have no matching vocal part in their charted passages. In particular, later words to Dvořák’s Largo are a separate work.
- *Mulher Rendeira* remains without words: the existing chart does not establish which historical lyric variant it uses, and the widely distributed film version contains Zé do Norte additions. Its lyric provenance needs to be settled before adding that text. See the [documented traditional/adapted distinction](https://auniao.pb.gov.br/servicos/correio-das-artes/edicao-digital-2023/correio-abril-2023.pdf).
- The Example Artist exercises do not have a corresponding sourced vocal line. Titles awaiting permission remain tracked in [RIGHTS_PENDING.md](RIGHTS_PENDING.md).

## Maintenance and validation

`.tools/lyrics.json` contains checked-in text, sources, zero-based onset indices and the expected notes/passes for each excerpt. All three chart generators apply the same index and reject changed melodies or pass lengths rather than silently attaching old words to a different tune. After editing entries, run:

```sh
python .tools/chart_lyrics.py
python .tools/test_lyrics.py
../harmonicon/target/debug/validate-pack .
```

Generators and lyric application need no network. The pack validator and lyric tests pass. Comparison with the original charts confirms unchanged pitches, timing, hole/action choices and musical metadata, apart from the required format-version bump. Regeneration preserves annotations. Physical singing/play-through and visual in-game review are still pending.
