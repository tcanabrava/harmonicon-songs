# Little Brown Jug

Plan ID: JZ03. Research date: 2026-10-02.

Underlying melody: Joseph Eastburn Winner, 1869; composer lived 1837–1918.

Melody reference: [Digital Tradition monophonic ABC](https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=back.numachi.com:8000/dtrad/abc_dtrad.tar.gz/abc_dtrad/BROWNJUG/0000). The 1869 composition and its named composer are beyond the life-plus-70 term in Luxembourg/EU and Brazil, and the original publication is beyond the US publication term. [FluteTunes catalog](https://www.flutetunes.com/tunes.php?id=10) corroborates composer and dates. Only the underlying refrain is used.

Adaptation: Refrain only, A to C in the upper register (F5–E6). All rhythmic values doubled so sixteenths become slow eighth notes. No big-band riffs or accompaniment.

The chart uses a standard C Richter harmonica, 72 BPM, 2/4, C, single notes only, and no bends. A four-beat count-in precedes the first note; repeated passes have two beats of breathing space. Upper-register exercises need musical and physical play-through before release.
