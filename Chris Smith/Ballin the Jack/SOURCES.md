# Ballin the Jack

Plan ID: JZ05. Research date: 2026-10-03.

Underlying melody: Chris Smith (1879–1949), 1913; lyricist Jim Burris (1876–1946).

Melody reference: [Levy original 1913 edition](https://levysheetmusic.mse.jhu.edu/collection/151/060). Printed page 4, third system, vocal melody only. Original 1913 publication; both credited authors died over 70 years ago. This chart excludes lyrics, piano harmony and later versions.

Adaptation: Chorus bars 5–6, ending before the chromatic pickup into bar 7; B-flat transposed to C in the upper register. Dotted-eighth/sixteenth pairs straightened to two eighths. Three passes.

The chart uses a standard C Richter harmonica, 68 BPM, 4/4, C, single notes only, and no bends. A four-beat count-in precedes the first note; repeated passes have two beats of breathing space. Upper-register exercises need musical and physical play-through before release.
